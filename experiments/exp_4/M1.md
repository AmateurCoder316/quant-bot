# EXP-4-M1

## Purpose

EXP-4 changes the information set and the trading horizon rather than merely changing model capacity.

It is a simulation/research experiment. It does not place real orders.

## Universe

### Target stocks

30 liquid large-cap equities spanning technology, communication services, consumer discretionary, financials, energy, health care, industrials and consumer staples.

### Context-only instruments

- SPY
- QQQ
- IWM
- XLK, XLC, XLY, XLF, XLE, XLV, XLI, XLP, XLU, XLRE, XLB

Context ETFs are never traded by EXP-4-M1. They provide market/sector/regime features only.

## Data

- Source: Alpaca IEX 5-minute bars
- Research bars: six complete regular-session 5-minute bars are combined into one 30-minute bar
- No premarket or after-hours bars
- No incomplete 30-minute candles
- Entry: next 30-minute bar open
- Main exit: open eight 30-minute bars after entry (4 hours)
- Main labels must enter and exit in the same regular session
- Commission: 0.10% per side
- Slippage: 0.05% per side

## Feature families

1. Per-stock price action, trend, volatility, volume and intraday structure
2. 30-stock breadth and cross-sectional ranks
3. SPY / QQQ / IWM returns, volatility and trend context
4. Each stock's sector ETF return, volatility and trend context
5. Stock-vs-SPY and stock-vs-sector relative strength
6. Simple market-regime flags
7. One-hot stock identity

## Model

Residual feed-forward neural network with:

- width search: 384 / 512 / 640
- 2 / 3 / 4 residual blocks
- GELU activations
- LayerNorm
- dropout
- AdamW
- smooth-L1 return regression
- pairwise ranking loss on the 4-hour target
- economically selected checkpoints

The model predicts 2-hour and 4-hour after-cost returns. The 4-hour head drives execution.

## Chronology

- Model search: 2023-2024 only
- Purged expanding validation blocks: 2024
- First forward model evaluation: 2025
- 2025 may select only the causal execution percentile
- Frozen shadow test: 2026

After 2025 is exposed, EXP-4-M1 must not be retrained or modified under the same model name.

## Execution

- max 3 simultaneous positions
- max 20% allocation per position
- causal rolling score threshold
- candidate percentiles: top 10%, 7.5%, 5%, 2.5%, 1%
- all stocks at a timestamp are judged against scores from strictly earlier timestamps
- no overnight positions

## Important limitation

The 30-stock universe is a deliberately chosen present-day liquid large-cap research universe, not a historical point-in-time index membership dataset. Results may therefore contain universe-selection/survivorship bias. A future experiment intended for stronger evidence should use point-in-time universe membership and delisting-aware data.
